What this is and isn't: the "Actual" and "Current" columns are plain historical fact from this regular season. The "Chance of..." columns estimate the odds of at least one such streak over each team's remaining regular-season games, using the team's own win rate so far and treating every game as independent of the last. That's a simplification — it ignores schedule strength, injuries and momentum — and with only 17 games per team, early-season win rates are noisy. Read it as rough math, not a forecast. Ties count as half a win in the win rate and break any streak.

Close-game record

Each team's record in one-score games — decided by 8 points or fewer — next to its record in everything else and its overall point differential. This is the football counterpart to the baseball closer table: it shows who has been finishing tight games. Analysts generally treat close-game records as heavily luck-driven, so a team winning far more close games than its point differential suggests is often viewed as a regression candidate. Shown as season-long context, not folded into any pick.
