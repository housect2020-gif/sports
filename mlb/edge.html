What this is: a filter, not a prediction model. Every game this season is classified by how decisive the head-to-head record between the two teams is — a real 3+ game H2H lead ("Strong"), a 1-2 game lead ("Moderate"), or a tied series decided only by season-record tiebreak ("Coinflip"). On top of that, tonight's starting pitchers are checked: on a Strong or Moderate pick, it's flagged if the better-pitching starter is actually going the other way (backtested: this widens or narrows the real odds noticeably on both tiers, more so on Moderate); on a Coinflip or No-data game, a genuinely large gap between the two starters' recent ERA (2.00+ over the last 90 days) can promote it to a usable "Pitcher edge" pick. The H2H backtest numbers below are computed live against every completed game this season, so they update as the season goes — the pitcher-edge rate (~72%) is from a one-time test and is kept visually distinct rather than presented as equally verified. Stacking multiple legs into a parlay multiplies the odds against you even when each individual pick is strong — see the note under "Combos" before reading too much into the combined percentage. Not real sportsbook odds, not betting advice — entertainment only.

A "great day" like an 8-in-a-row or 13-in-a-row streak is real when it happens, but it isn't a repeatable signal you can spot in advance; it's ordinary variance around these season-wide averages.
