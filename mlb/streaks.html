What this is and isn't: the "Actual" columns are plain historical fact — the real longest win/loss streaks each team has had this season. The "Chance of..." columns are a genuine probability estimate for the rest of the season, computed from each team's own season win rate, assuming games are independent of one another (i.e. a win or loss today doesn't change tomorrow's odds). That assumption isn't a guess — we backtested it directly earlier this season and found essentially no game-to-game correlation (50.3% vs 49.7%), so treating each remaining game as an independent coin flip weighted by that team's real win rate is a reasonable, honest model — not a guarantee of what will actually happen, just the math given what's true so far.

Closer reliability

Each team's primary closer this season (by saves) and how often they've actually converted a save opportunity vs. blown it. This only matters once a game reaches a real save situation — a late lead of 3 runs or fewer — which is why it's shown here as season-long reference, not folded into any specific game's pick. Backtested: this season's top-10 closers by saves blow only about 8% of opportunities, vs. about 27% for the bottom 10 — a real, sizeable gap. Teams without an established closer in the league's top 30 by saves aren't shown; that itself is informative — no proven closer yet.
