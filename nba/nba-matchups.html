How to read this

BET = the rule and the model agree and the model is 3+ points from the sportsbook total. LEAN = they agree but the model is closer. SKIP = they disagree. NO EDGE = the model is within 1 point of the book. The rule: favorite at home → Under, favorite on the road → Over. The model total is each team’s scoring plus its 4th-quarter habits by matchup type.
